# PaySwitch Professional Code of Conduct

The PaySwitch community brings together financial engineers, cryptographers, software developers, and fintech researchers working on sovereign payment systems.

---

## 1. Professional Principles

All participants, contributors, and maintainers commit to:

- **Technical Excellence:** Uphold rigorous mathematical precision, software verification, and systems reliability.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Critique technical design, code, and architecture on merit, facts, and benchmarks rather than personal attribution.
- **Financial Responsibility:** Recognize the critical responsibility inherent in authoring software that manages real-world financial balances and economic settlement.
- **Integrity and Transparency:** Disclose potential architectural trade-offs, performance limitations, and security assumptions honestly.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in PaySwitch communication channels, issue trackers, and pull requests:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal disparagement.
- Deliberate introduction of obfuscated logic, backdoors, or unverified financial mutations.
- Trolling, bad-faith debate, or disruption of project discussions.

---

## 3. Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the authority to moderate discussions, reject pull requests, and restrict repository access to individuals who fail to uphold these standards.

Inquiries or concerns regarding conduct should be submitted directly to `rahadbhuiya2021@gmail.com`.
